What Is Rainwater Harvesting?

Rainwater harvesting is the process of collecting and storing rainwater for future use instead of allowing all of it to flow away.

💧

Save Water

Collect rainwater to reduce dependence on treated water for suitable household uses.

🌱

Protect Nature

Thoughtful rainwater management can help conserve water resources and manage runoff.

🏠

Use Your Roof

A rooftop, gutters, filters, and a storage tank can help collect useful rainwater.

How Rainwater Harvesting Works

1

Collect

Rain falls onto the roof.

2

Channel

Gutters direct the water into pipes.

3

Filter and Store

Filter debris and store water safely.

4

Reuse

Use water for suitable purposes.
